Privacy Policy for ySpend

Last updated: September 27, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what data the ySpend mobile application ("ySpend", "the app", "we") collects, how it is used, and your choices around it. ySpend is a personal expense- and income-tracking app.

No account, no login

ySpend does not require you to create an account or sign in. There is no username, password, or personal identity tied to your use of the app by us.

Your financial data stays on your device

All the financial information you enter — expenses, income, categories, sellers, payment methods, recurring entries, and any notes — is stored locally on your device in a local database. We do not operate any server that receives, stores, or has access to this data. It is never transmitted to us.

Backups (iCloud / Google Drive)

ySpend offers an optional backup feature. If you enable it, a backup of your local data is saved to your own iCloud account (on iOS) or your own Google Drive account (on Android) — this is storage you control and that belongs to you, not to us. We do not have access to these backups. You can disable this feature at any time in Settings.

App unlock (Face ID / fingerprint / device passcode)

If you enable app lock in Settings, ySpend uses your device's built-in biometric or passcode authentication (Face ID, Touch ID, fingerprint, or device PIN) to unlock the app. This authentication is handled entirely by your device's operating system — we do not receive, see, or store your biometric data or passcode.

Analytics

ySpend uses Firebase Analytics (provided by Google) to understand aggregate, anonymized usage patterns — for example, which screens are viewed, or that an expense was added — so we can improve the app. This does not include the content of your financial entries (e.g., we may log that "an expense was added," not what the expense was, its amount, or its category).

You can opt out of analytics at any time via Settings → "Share usage data." Turning this off disables analytics collection going forward.

Subscriptions and payments

ySpend offers an optional Pro subscription (monthly or yearly), processed entirely through Apple's App Store or Google Play's billing systems. We do not receive or store your payment card details — those are handled directly by Apple or Google under their own privacy policies. We may receive a purchase receipt/confirmation from the app store to verify your subscription status.

Data we do not collect

  • We do not collect your name, email address, or phone number.
  • We do not access your contacts, location, camera, or microphone.
  • We do not sell or share your data with third parties for advertising.
  • ySpend does not display third-party ads.

Third-party services

ySpend relies on the following third-party services, each governed by its own privacy policy:

  • Google Firebase (Analytics) — Firebase Privacy Policy
  • Apple iCloud (optional backups on iOS) — Apple Privacy Policy
  • Google Drive (optional backups on Android) — Google Privacy Policy
  • Apple App Store / Google Play Billing (subscriptions) — governed by the respective store's privacy policy

Children's privacy

ySpend is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Data deletion

Since your data is stored locally on your device, you can delete it at any time by clearing the app's data or uninstalling the app. If you've enabled cloud backups, you can also delete those backup files directly from your iCloud or Google Drive account.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.
